Engineering Leadership

Marvin Guerra

Currently serving as Head of Engineering at Pulley, where I focus on team development, product strategy, and engineering leadership to deliver scalable equity management solutions. My career has taken me through leadership roles across AI, Growth, Payments, and Infrastructure at companies like Figma, Webflow, Fast, Quibi, and GoFundMe.

Along the way, I've advised early-stage startups, championed Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion, and built software systems spanning event pipelines, experimentation platforms, payments, and data warehousing.

I'm a proud immigrant from Peru 🇵🇪 and a first-generation MIT 🦫 graduate, experiences that shape how I lead, build, and connect. Outside of work, I'm powered by good coffee ☕ and driven by the rhythms of salsa, reggaeton, and hip-hop 🎧.

I'm here not just for work, but for community, creativity, and meaningful collaboration. Let's build something great together.

What I bring

Expertise
01

Product-Led Growth

I grow products by pairing user insight with steady experimentation. At Webflow I owned the full acquisition and activation funnels and helped design the A/B testing and analytics platform behind them. At Figma I turned community publishing for teams and organizations into a real growth channel.

+20% user acquisition at Webflow

02

Building Teams

I build teams that keep their quality as they grow. At Pulley I wrote the headcount plan and every job description, rebuilt the interview loop, and developed three tech leads in my first five months. Before that I grew Webflow Growth from 3 to 12 engineers and Figma Editor AI from 1 to 4.

3x engineering org at Pulley, from 8 to 24

03

Shipping, Zero to Launch

I take cross-functional work from a blank page to launch. At Figma my team shipped AI features in under four months. At Pulley I brought in planning, retros, and team health checks, then delivered 85% of a quarter's work on time while scope grew 40%.

85% on time across 27 initiatives in one quarter

04

Systems That Scale

I've built the systems I now lead teams on: subscription billing at Quibi, payments and search at GoFundMe, event pipelines at Fast, and ML features in Figma's real-time editor. That hands-on depth earns trust with engineers and keeps the architecture tied to what the business needs.

5M+ sign-ups on Quibi's billing system
